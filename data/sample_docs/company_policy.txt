REFUND AND RETURN POLICY

Customers may return any item within 30 days of the original purchase date.
To process a return, the original receipt or order confirmation is required.
Items must be in their original packaging and unused condition.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
Digital products and downloadable software are non-refundable.

SHIPPING POLICY

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days and is available for $4.99.
Free standard shipping is offered on orders over $50.
Express shipping (1-2 business days) is available for $14.99.
Next-day delivery is available in select regions for $24.99.
International shipping is available to 45 countries. Delivery takes 10-21 days.

PRIVACY POLICY

We collect only the data necessary to process your order.
Customer data is never sold to third parties.
You may request deletion of your personal data at any time.
We use industry-standard encryption for all transactions.
Cookies are used to improve your browsing experience.
