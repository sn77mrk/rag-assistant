Competitor Company Policy
Version 1.4 — Updated March 2024

RETURNS AND REFUNDS

Our return window is 14 days from the date of delivery.
Items must be unopened and in original condition to qualify for a refund.
A return shipping fee of $5.99 is deducted from all refunds.
Refund processing takes 7-10 business days.
Store credit is offered as an alternative with an additional 10% bonus.

DELIVERY OPTIONS

Standard delivery (7-10 business days) is free for all orders.
Priority delivery (3-5 business days) costs $9.99.
Same-day delivery is available in metropolitan areas for $19.99.
No international shipping is currently offered.

DATA AND PRIVACY

We collect purchase history and browsing behavior for analytics.
Anonymized data may be shared with marketing partners.
Users can opt out of data sharing via their account settings.
Data retention period is 3 years from last activity.

CONTACT AND SUPPORT

Customer service is available 7 days a week, 8am to 10pm EST.
Email: help@competitor.com — response within 48 hours.
No phone or live chat support available.
